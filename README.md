# AI Operations Proposal - Snow Ball Hospital Group

Technical report evaluating whether a paediatric hospital group should adopt an AI-driven scheduling and operations system. 5-person group project, CSC1036 Professional Practice for Computing, DCU (2026).

📄 [Read the full report (PDF)](report.pdf)

## Overview
Working as a consulting team, we analysed the hospital group's operational problems (fragmented systems, long wait times, underused theatres), evaluated an AI scheduling solution, assessed its feasibility and risks, and designed an 18-month phased rollout.

## My contribution
- Wrote **Section 7 - Ethical, Legal and Governance Considerations**: EU AI Act (high-risk classification), GDPR and the Irish Data Protection Act, HIQA standards, algorithmic bias, human oversight, cybersecurity (zero-trust, encryption, data retention) and governance and audit structures
- Created the ethics, legal and governance presentation slide and presented the final recommendation

**Topics:** AI strategy, responsible AI, EU AI Act, GDPR, risk assessment, IT governance
